# Report v2 Review Protocol

## Objective
Review whether the Second Report:
1. Correctly integrates old and new PDFs
2. Preserves citation integrity
3. Avoids duplication
4. Follows structural instructions
5. Maintains academic rigor

---

## Review Scope

### Input Files
- first_report/report_v1.pdf
- /existing_pdfs
- /downloaded_pdfs
- instructions.md
- citation_rules.md
- report_outline.md
- integration_strategy.md
- source_mapping.md
- draft report_v2

---

## Section 1 — Structural Compliance

Checklist:
- Does report_v2 follow report_outline.md exactly?
- Are all required sections present?
- Is literature review split into old vs new research?
- Are comparative discussions included?

Mark:
[ ] Pass
[ ] Needs revision
[ ] Major structural deviation

---

## Section 2 — Citation Integrity Audit

Verify:
- All v1 citations preserved
- No renumbering errors
- No deleted references without justification
- New references added sequentially
- No duplicate references
- In-text citations match bibliography

Check:
- Citation formatting consistency
- DOI correctness (if applicable)
- Author-year consistency (if APA)

Mark:
[ ] Clean
[ ] Minor formatting issues
[ ] Citation corruption detected

---

## Section 3 — Content Integration Quality

Assess:
- Are new PDFs genuinely integrated?
- Or merely appended?
- Are overlapping themes merged?
- Are contradictions addressed?
- Is synthesis present?

Mark:
[ ] Strong synthesis
[ ] Partial integration
[ ] Weak integration

---

## Section 4 — Redundancy Detection

Identify:
- Repeated arguments
- Repeated paragraphs
- Same claim cited separately without merging

Mark:
[ ] No redundancy
[ ] Moderate redundancy
[ ] High redundancy

---

## Section 5 — Academic Strength

Evaluate:
- Argument depth improved?
- Newer research strengthens claims?
- Logical progression maintained?
- Technical accuracy preserved?

Mark:
[ ] Improved
[ ] Same level
[ ] Weakened

---

## Section 6 — Risk Flags

Flag if found:
- Hallucinated references
- Unsupported claims
- Fabricated DOIs
- Citations not present in PDFs
- Logical contradictions
