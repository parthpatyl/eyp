## New Sources (Citations [39]–[58])

### Perpetual-Flight Demonstrations
39. **Oettershagen et al. (2017)** – AtlantikSolar 81-hour world record flight
   - PDF: `JFR_81hFlight_paper_final.pdf`
   - Key Topics: Charge-margin methodology, CDAF framework, meteorological robustness

### Empirical HALE Programmes
40. **Hwang et al. (2016)** – EAV-3 Korean solar HALE UAV aerodynamic design
   - PDF: `JAKO201614652759665.pdf`
   - Key Topics: Wing bending, low cruise speed effects, static margin validation

### Hybrid Propulsion Concepts
41. **Mermer & Özgen (2017)** – Hybrid turbofan/solar HALE UAV
   - PDF: `EUCASS2017-200.pdf`
   - Key Topics: Competitor analysis, structural weight models, hybrid mission profiles

### Propulsion Optimization
42. **Dantsker et al. (2020)** – Solar UAV propulsion optimization
   - PDF: `SolarUAVPropulsionOptimization_AIAA-PropEnergy-2020.pdf`
   - Key Topics: Motor-propeller matching, 19% efficiency improvement, mission-based optimization

### Photovoltaic Integration
43. **Łopusiewicz & Książek (2024)** – PV cell integration on composite wings
   - PDF: `icas2024_1132_paper.pdf`
   - Key Topics: Lamination methods, micro-cracking, galvanic isolation, manufacturing defects

### Advanced Aeroelasticity
44. **Murua et al. (2011)** – Very flexible aircraft stability with free-wake effects
   - PDF: `Stability and Open-Loop Dynamics...Murua2011-1915.pdf`
   - Key Topics: UVLM-based coupled analysis, wake interference, geometrically exact beam models

### Alternative Energy Paradigms
45. **Liller et al. (2025)** – Battery-free solar UAV with supercapacitors
   - PDF: `s41598-025-90729-2.pdf` or `Battery_free_UAVs_Nature_2024.pdf`
