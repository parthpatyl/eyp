## Structural Simulations for CFRP Specimen

**Material:** Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP)
**Dimensions:** 100 mm × 50 mm × 0.5 mm

---

### 1. Tensile Testing

* Uniaxial tensile test
* Longitudinal tensile strength (0° fiber direction)
* Transverse tensile strength (90° direction)
* Young’s modulus (E₁, E₂)
* Poisson’s ratio (ν₁₂)
* Stress–strain curve extraction

---

### 2. Compressive Testing

* Uniaxial compression test
* Longitudinal compressive strength
* Transverse compressive strength
* Buckling tendency under compressive load

---

### 3. Flexural Testing

* Three-point bending simulation
* Four-point bending simulation
* Flexural strength
* Flexural modulus
* Deflection behavior

---

### 4. Shear Testing

* In-plane shear test
* Interlaminar shear strength (ILSS)
* Short beam shear test
